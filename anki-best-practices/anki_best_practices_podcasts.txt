For a podcast episode card, what should the question include?	The episode title or number and the exact topic being tested.
How should you handle guest information?	Ask for the guest name and their role or expertise in the episode.
What is a good way to test a key takeaway?	Ask for the specific insight or recommendation attributed to a speaker.
How can you avoid vague prompts about an episode summary?	Ask about one concrete claim or story discussed in the episode.
What should you do with multi-part discussions?	Split them into separate cards for each distinct point.
How should you ask about advice or steps given in an episode?	Ask for one step and its purpose or outcome.
What is the preferred way to test definitions introduced in a podcast?	Ask for the term and the exact definition given by the speaker.
How should you handle statistics mentioned in an episode?	Ask for the number and what it refers to in that episode.
What should be included when testing a quote from a guest?	The speaker and the context or topic of the quote.
How should you differentiate similar episodes or series?	Include the series name and episode identifier in the question.
What is a good way to test an anecdote?	Ask for the key point or lesson drawn from the anecdote.
How should you handle follow-up actions mentioned in an episode?	Make a card for each action and the reason it was suggested.
