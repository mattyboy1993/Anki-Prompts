For a movie plot card, what should the question specify?	The exact scene or event and the character involved.
How should you handle a movie’s themes in cards?	Ask about one theme tied to a specific scene or character decision.
What is a good way to test a character’s motivation?	Ask for the motivation behind a named action or scene.
How can you make setting questions precise?	Ask about a named location and its role in a particular scene.
What should a card about a plot twist avoid?	Vague prompts like “What is the twist?” without context.
How should you handle similar characters across movies or sequels?	Name the movie and the character to disambiguate.
What is a good way to create cards about cinematography or style?	Ask for a specific technique used in a named scene and its effect.
How should you ask about a movie’s ending?	Ask about one concrete outcome or decision in the final sequence.
What is the preferred way to test a movie’s central conflict?	Ask for the conflict between specific parties or goals.
How should you handle quotable lines from a film?	Ask who said the line or what situation it appears in, one fact per card.
What should you do with multiple subplots?	Create separate cards for each subplot’s key event or resolution.
How should you test a director’s intent or message?	Ask for the stated or implied message tied to a specific scene.
