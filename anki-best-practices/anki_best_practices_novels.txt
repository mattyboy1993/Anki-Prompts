For a novel character card, what should the question specify?	The character name and the exact role or relationship being tested.
What is a good way to ask about a novel’s setting?	Ask for a specific place or time period tied to a named event or chapter.
How should you handle a novel’s themes in Anki cards?	Turn each theme into a focused question with a concrete example from the story.
What should a plot event card avoid?	Broad prompts like “What happens in the novel?” that test multiple events.
How can you make a novel card about symbolism unambiguous?	Name the symbol and ask for its specific meaning or function in the story.
When asking about a novel’s conflict, what should be included?	The protagonists and the exact nature of the conflict being tested.
What is the preferred way to test a character arc?	Ask about a specific change in the character tied to a clear moment.
How should you treat secondary characters in cards?	Focus on one clear role or action rather than a full biography.
What is a good prompt style for memorable quotes?	Ask who said the quote and in what context, one fact per card.
How should you handle multiple plotlines?	Create separate cards for each plotline and each key event.
What should be done if two characters are easily confused?	Explicitly contrast them in the question to prevent ambiguity.
How should you ask about a novel’s ending?	Ask about a single, specific outcome rather than summarizing the whole ending.
